📋 Race Day Checklist

  • Race bib & safety pins
  • GPS watch + charger
  • Running shoes (worn in!)
  • Race-day outfit tested
  • Energy gels (3-4 for race)
  • Water bottle or plan aid stations
  • Phone (for emergencies)
  • Toilet paper (just in case)
  • Warm clothes for after
  • Recovery snack
💡 Put everything out the night before!

🔁 Taper (2-3 weeks before)

  • Reduce mileage by 20-30% each week
  • Keep intensity low — easy runs only
  • Don't try anything new
  • Sleep more, stress less
  • Final long run = 2-3 weeks out, max 16km

🏃 8-Week Beginner Plan

W1-2: 3 runs/week, 5-8km easy
W3-4: 4 runs, long run 12-14km
W5-6: 4 runs, long run 16-20km
W7: Long run 22km, then cut back
W8: Taper — easy 8km, rest
🏃 Long runs should be slow! Heart rate zone 2.

💪 Form Tips

  • Cadence: 170-180 steps/min
  • Posture: Slight forward lean, not from waist
  • Arms: 90° angle, swing lightly
  • Footstrike: Land under hip, midfoot
  • Breathing: 3:3 in/out through nose if possible

🍌 Race Day Nutrition

Night Before
  • Carb-heavy (pasta, rice)
  • Low fat, low fiber
  • Water, not alcohol
Race Morning
  • 2-3 hours before
  • Familiar food (toast, banana)
  • 500ml water

⚡ During the Race

  • Start fueling at km 30 (not 40!)
  • 1 gel every 30-40 min
  • Drink water at every station
  • Don't try anything new!
☀️ Hot race? Drink more, add electrolytes.

👟 Shoe Replacement

🩰 Shoe Types

  • Neutral: Most runners, good arch support
  • Stability: Overpronation (feet roll in)
  • Carbon: Race day only, faster but firm
  • Trail: Off-road, more grip
👟 Replace at 800-1000km for injury prevention.

📋 Race Day Checklist

  • Race bib & safety pins
  • GPS watch + charger
  • Running shoes (worn in!)
  • Race-day outfit tested
  • Energy gels (3-4)
  • Water bottle or plan aid stations
  • Phone (emergencies)
  • Warm clothes for after
  • Recovery snack
💡 Put everything out the night before!

🔁 Taper (2-3 weeks before)

  • Reduce mileage by 20-30%
  • Easy runs only
  • Don't try anything new
  • Sleep more, stress less
  • Final long run 2-3 weeks out

🏃 8-Week Beginner Plan

W1-2: 3 runs/week, 5-8km easy
W3-4: 4 runs, long run 12-14km
W5-6: 4 runs, long run 16-20km
W7: Long run 22km, then cut back
W8: Taper — easy 8km, rest
🏃 Long runs should be slow! HR zone 2.

💪 Form Tips

  • Cadence: 170-180 steps/min
  • Posture: Slight forward lean
  • Arms: 90° angle, swing lightly
  • Breathing: 3:3 in/out

🍌 Race Day Nutrition

Night Before
  • Carb-heavy
  • Low fat, low fiber
  • Water, not alcohol
Race Morning
  • 2-3 hours before
  • Familiar food
  • 500ml water

⚡ During the Race

  • Start fueling at km 30
  • 1 gel every 30-40 min
  • Drink water at every station
  • Don't try anything new!
☀️ Hot race? Drink more + electrolytes.

👟 Shoe Replacement

🩰 Shoe Types

  • Neutral: Most runners
  • Stability: Overpronation
  • Carbon: Race day only
  • Trail: Off-road
👟 Replace at 800-1000km.
